word.svg, excel.svg, powerpoint.svg, outlook.svg and teams.svg are unmodified
copies from the toolkit's "M365 app icon SVG's.zip".
copilot.svg is the unmodified "Copilot-icon-filled-gradient-rgb.svg".
Filenames are normalised; artwork, colours, viewBox and proportions are unchanged.

Use only to identify the corresponding Microsoft product in the Apps row.
Cowork uses the Copilot app icon because it is a feature of that app, not a
separately branded app. Surfaces without a sourced product icon retain the
existing generic Fluent UI symbol.

These are Microsoft brand assets, not MIT-licensed Fluent UI system icons.

library's logo or imply Microsoft endorsement of this community library.
